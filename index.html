    FAQ

    Ask the baker

    Who gets a slice?

    The top 10 $SLICE holders at each snapshot. Every 5 minutes, on the UTC clock, we bake 100% of the creator fees claimed in that window into one pie and slice it among them.

    How big is my slice?

    Proportional to your balance within the top 10. Your slice = your $SLICE ÷ the combined $SLICE of all top-10 wallets. Hold a quarter of that total, get a quarter of the pie.

    Which wallets don't count?

    The pump.fun bonding curve, LP pools (Raydium, Meteora, PumpSwap), burn addresses and the treasury itself. Only real holder wallets get a seat at the table.

    How much of the fees go to holders?

    100% of claimed creator fees. No team cut. Everything is visible on-chain from the treasury wallet above.

    Do I need to claim anything?

    No. If you're in the top 10 at bake time, the SOL lands in your wallet automatically. Anyone DMing you to "claim" is a scammer.

    Does this site touch my wallet?

    Never. It only reads public blockchain data. It will never ask you to connect, sign or send anything.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. $SLICE is a memecoin with no intrinsic value and no expectation of profit. Only ape what you can afford to lose.
